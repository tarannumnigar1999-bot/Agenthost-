Copy and paste this snippet into any website. Your agent will appear as a chat widget. Replace YOUR_AGENT_ID with your actual agent ID.
<script src="https://agenthost.io/widget.js" data-agent="YOUR_AGENT_ID" data-theme="dark" data-position="bottom-right" defer ></script>
